There are two main things that you need to evaluate:
1. Semantic score: Does the translation process from the origin text to the translated

shift of meaning and the translated result doesn’t even make sense that it will lean
more towards the number 1. If it keeps the meaning of the question really well that it

2. Entity translation score: Does the translation process still keep the entities in the
origin text? For example the word Software Laboratory Center it is an entity and
should not be changed after the translation process, then the score will lean more
towards the number 1.
